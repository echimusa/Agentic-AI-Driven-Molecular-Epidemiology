# Licensing

| What | Where | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Code | `chapters/*/code/`, `chapters/*/review/` (the check scripts), `scripts/`, `tools/`, `r-packages.R`, `requirements.txt`, `.github/` | MIT, see [LICENSE](LICENSE) |
| Text, figures and tables | `chapters/*/figures/`, `bibliography/`, `docs/`, the READMEs | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), see [LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt](LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt) |
| Data | `chapters/*/data/` | **Not covered by either licence.** The files are public data or derived from public data and keep the terms of their sources; see [docs/DATA.md](docs/DATA.md), which records the terms as understood and is not legal advice |
