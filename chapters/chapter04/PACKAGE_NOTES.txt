PROVENANCE AND LIMITS
- NCBI re-runs the Pathogen Detection analysis and retires old versions (accessions are not archival). The chapter uses Listeria version PDG000000001.4985, downloaded 13 November 2026; a later download gives different numbers.
- Clusters in the public table are single-linkage clusters at 50 SNPs; the table holds only isolates that entered a cluster. "Opposite type" is the pipeline's label for the isolation source (clinical against environmental).
- The SNP-versus-allele analysis is a simulation (independent mutations, one recombination tract per event, 60% of SNPs in scheme loci, 1,700 loci); it is not a real cgMLST analysis.
- Chapter_4_3.py as uploaded fails because it applies XOR to a floating-point array; ex4_1 stores integers. Chapter_4_4.R needs gplots for its heatmap; ex4_2 uses base R.
- The implied time to a common ancestor uses a published Listeria rate (2.5e-7 per site per year) and the 2.86 Mb aligned in the data; it is a derived figure.
- Every DOI was resolved against Crossref on 13 November 2026. The Word file renders to 13 A4 pages in LibreOffice; it was not opened in Word, and the style-XML schema check of the validator reports errors on pandoc-generated files.
- The uploaded draft was used for its structure (SNP and cgMLST paradigms, workflow diagram, comparison table); its cut-off ranges and its statement about autonomous threshold adjustment were checked against the literature and replaced (see the chapter), and its reference list was corrected.
