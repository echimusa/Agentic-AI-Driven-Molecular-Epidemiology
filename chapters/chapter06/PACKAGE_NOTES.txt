HOW TO RUN
Exercise 1 (Python 3.12). pip install biopython mappy numpy pandas scipy matplotlib
    cd code; cp ../data/sars2.fasta ../data/sars2_meta.csv .; python ex6_root_to_tip.py
  Expected: 327 genomes; slope 6.59e-04 per site per year (SE 1.4e-05) = 19.7 per genome per year; R2 = 0.87; x-intercept 17 Nov 2019;
  date randomisation one-sided p = 0.001. Writes sars2_root_to_tip_table.csv and figs6/. Without the two data files the script downloads a fresh sample
  from NCBI GenBank, which gives similar but not identical numbers.
Exercise 2 (R 4.3 with ggplot2). cd code; Rscript ex6_skyline.R
  Expected: one genealogy (seed 42) 0.39 per year by skyline regression and 0.42 by maximum likelihood; 200 genealogies 0.46 (SD 0.04) and 0.51 (SD 0.04), true 0.50.
Exercise 3 (TreeTime 0.12.1, pip install phylo-treetime; FastTree 2.1.11 on the path; about 25 minutes from scratch).
    cd code; cp ../data/ebola_* ../data/sars2_* .; python ex6_treetime.py
  Writes table_treetime.csv, table_treetime_drt.csv, table_skyline_ebola.csv and figs6/fig_ch6_timetree.png. A second call reuses the runs in tt_runs/ and the shuffle table;
  RERUN=1 repeats everything. Delete sars2_tree.nwk to rebuild the SARS-CoV-2 tree with FastTree.
  Expected (covariation): Ebola 17.6 +/- 3.4 substitutions per genome-year, root 9 Mar 2014 (22 Jan to 21 Apr 2014); SARS-CoV-2 16.6 +/- 1.2, root 12 Sep 2019 (30 Jul to 4 Nov 2019).
Exercise 4 (Python). cd code; python ex6_time_dependent.py   (about 10 seconds)
  Expected: apparent rate 49.6, 46.3, 27.3, 12.0 and 10.2 per genome-year at 1, 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000 years; a 10-year rate dates a 1,000-year divergence at 259 years.
Exercise 5 (BEAST 2.7.7 with its bundled Java runtime, and the MODEL_SELECTION package 1.6.2; about 90 minutes on one processor).
    Install BEAST 2.7.7 and put its bin folder on the path. If the package manager cannot reach a repository, download
    https://github.com/BEAST2-Dev/model-selection/releases/download/v1.6.0/MODEL_SELECTION.addon.v1.6.2.zip and unzip it into ~/.beast/2.7/MODEL_SELECTION/.
    cd code; cp ../data/ebola_aln.fasta ../data/ebola_dates.csv ../data/ebola_tree.nwk .
    python ex6_beast.py make --mcmc-length 6000000 --ps-steps 20 --ps-length 250000 --ps-pre 100000
    ./run_all.sh            # skips runs that finished; a log is copied only after the run that wrote it
    python ex6_beast_analysis.py
  Expected: strict clock 27.8 substitutions per genome-year (95% HPD 21.2 to 34.4), common ancestor on 27 April 2014; log Bayes factors of the real-date strict model of 49.3 against no dates and
  44.4 to 47.8 against three randomisations of the dates (stepping stone), 2.5 against the relaxed clock. Chains with the same seeds should give the same logs on the same BEAST 2 version,
  but the analyses were not repeated from scratch for this package. The analysis script needs the full BEAST 2 output (step folders), which run_all.sh creates;
  the shipped ps_step_samples.csv.gz holds the step samples that validate_ch6.py uses.

PROVENANCE AND LIMITS
- Sections 7 (Omicron) and 9 (Zika), and the tuberculosis-complex paragraph of Section 8, summarise published analyses. The Omicron common-ancestor date (early October 2021)
  is taken from the institutional press summary of the paper. The calibration, time-dependence and circularity material follows Soares and Rito (2026), Biology 15:1329 (open access, CC BY).
- Sections 6 (MRSA) and 8 (TB-MDR scenario) are illustrative teaching scenarios; their numbers are not real results.
- Exercise 5 is a demonstration: 60 of the 195 Ebola genomes (every third by collection date), chains of 6 million steps (smallest effective sample sizes 132 for the strict and 106 for the relaxed clock),
  one marginal-likelihood run of 20 steps for each model, three randomisations of the dates. The effective sample size of the likelihood is below 100 at 17 to 20 of the 20 steps of each run, so the
  Bayes factors of 44 to 49 for the real dates are safe and the 1.0 to 2.5 for strict against relaxed clock is not. The estimate BEAST 2 prints at the end of a path-sampling run is the stepping-stone estimate.
- BEAST 2 and TreeTime disagree on the clock rate on the same 60 genomes (27.8 against 10.8 substitutions per genome-year); the cause was not tested.
- TreeTime intervals are conditional on one fixed tree; intervals for imposed rates reflect an assumed 20% standard deviation of the rate. Exercise 4 is a model with assumed parameters.
- Five of 50 Ebola shuffles and one of 30 SARS-CoV-2 shuffles could not be rooted with a positive rate; they count as rates below the observed one.
- The statement that reported Ebola cases kept rising through 2014 is general knowledge and is not cited in the chapter.
- References were checked against PubMed and Crossref on 8 November 2026; the 21 added on 14 and 15 November 2026 were resolved against Crossref.
- Equations in the Word file were verified by converting them back to LaTeX; they were not viewed in Word. Slides were rendered through LibreOffice and not opened in PowerPoint.
